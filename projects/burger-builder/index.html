Burger Builder

An Android app for building a burger. Tap an ingredient in the tray to slide it between the buns; the price updates as you go. It was written to show object-oriented programming in Kotlin.

  • A base burger costs $250.
  • Cheese adds $20, a patty $50 and onion $15. New layers stack right under the top bun.
  • Reset clears the layers and the price.
KotlinOOPAbstract classInterfaceRecyclerView
Download Kotlin source (.zip)

The original models ingredients with a Priceable interface, an abstract BurgerComponent class and an Ingredient subclass, and shows them in a horizontal RecyclerView. The "Let me order!" button has no action yet in the original app.
