Poison Game

A two-player, pass-and-play card game. Two poison cards are hidden in a 4×4 grid. Players take turns flipping cards, scoring a point for every candy. Flip a poison and the game ends.

  1. Enter both player names and press Start Game.
  2. The first two taps secretly hide the two poisons. Pass the device so each player hides one.
  3. Take turns flipping cards: 🍭 scores a point, ☠️ ends the game.
  4. Highest score wins once someone is poisoned or every card is flipped.
