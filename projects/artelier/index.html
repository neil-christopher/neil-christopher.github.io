Artelier (VisionaryAtelier)

The full Figma prototype for Artelier, an AI-free art platform. Artists build a virtual atelier, set up memberships, auctions and commissions, and post to their socials. Customers browse, follow and commission artists.

  • Start with the Artist or Customer journey, or jump to building an atelier.
  • Tap buttons and cards; some screens advance on their own like the original.
  • Tap empty space to flash what is clickable.
