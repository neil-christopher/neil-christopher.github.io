Face Change App

An Android app where you roll a random cartoon face, scramble it into four parts, then tap each part to rebuild the face. This web version runs the same flow in your browser with the app's original images.

  1. Press Roll to shuffle faces, then Stop to lock in a target.
  2. Press Start to fill the four parts with random pieces.
  3. Tap a part to swap it for the next piece until the face matches.
