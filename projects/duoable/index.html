Duo-Able

A two-player co-op puzzle game. Each player is on their own device and sees different clues, so the only way through is to talk. Three rounds: a slider lock, a radio-and-piano melody, and a story you have to piece together in order.

  • Choose Player 1 on one phone and Player 2 on the other.
  • Read your clues aloud; your partner can't see your screen.
  • Hit the red button when you think you've solved a round.
